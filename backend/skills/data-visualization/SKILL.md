# Data Visualization

Choose the smallest visual that makes the relationship easier to understand.
Use bars for comparisons, lines for change over time, scatter plots for
relationships, and tables for exact mappings. Label units and time ranges,
avoid decorative chart effects, and include accessible text alternatives.
